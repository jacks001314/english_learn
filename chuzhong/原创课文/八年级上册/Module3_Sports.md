## 对话（Dialogue）

**Jack:** Are you going to watch the football match tonight?

**Ben:** I am. It is the final, and the two teams are both very strong.

**Jack:** Which team do you think is better?

**Ben:** I think ours will win. They have a new coach, and they train harder than before.

**Jack:** But the other team beat us last time. That was careless of us.

**Ben:** We have practised plenty of times since then. Everyone is more confident now.

**Jack:** What is the score going to be?

**Ben:** I hope it will be two to one. Come and cheer the team on with us!

## 短文（Reading）

### A Better Player

Last year, Leo was a careless player. He was fast, but he never listened to his coach, and he often missed easy chances. His team always lost, and Leo felt sorry. He was not pleased with himself.

Then he decided to change. He began to do warm-up exercises every day, and he practised after school for an hour. His coach taught him how to pass the ball and how to play against strong teams. Slowly, Leo became more careful and more confident.

This year, his team is the best in the school. They have scored many goals, and Leo has scored more than anyone else. "Sports is not only about winning," Leo says. "It is about learning to work together. Playing football is exciting, but working with my team is even more enjoyable."

Now Leo is the most important player on the team. He does not miss his chances any more, and his teammates cheer him on loudly. This autumn, they will take part in the school Olympics. Leo says he is ready and he is not afraid.
