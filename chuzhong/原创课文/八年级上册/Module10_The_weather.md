# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 10 The weather

> 主题：天气（may/might）；词汇对齐：cloud / shower / snow / storm / cloudy / rainy / snowy / sunny / windy / skate / thick / ice / joke / might / temperature / minus / degree / although / wet / neither / terrible / wish / probably / come on / mile / round / northwest / southeast / from time to time

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Amy:** What are you doing this weekend, Tom?

**Tom:** I'm not sure. It depends on the weather.

**Amy:** Look at the clouds. It might rain, so we may not be able to go skating.

**Tom:** I saw the weather report. The temperature will probably go down to minus five degrees tonight. It's going to be very cold.

**Amy:** If the ice is thick enough, we can skate. But if it snows, the roads will be wet and terrible.

**Tom:** Let's wait and see. It may be snowy in the north, but it's usually sunny here.

**Amy:** Come on, we can't stay inside all day. If the weather is fine, it's a chance to go out.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Weather Changes

The weather is different in different places. In the northwest, the wind often blows for many days, and the sky is grey. In the southeast, there are showers from time to time, and it rains a lot in July.

Sometimes the weather changes in a strange way. In the morning, the sky is clear and sunny. At noon, dark clouds appear, and the sun disappears. Then the wind gets up, the temperature drops to a low point, and a storm comes suddenly. People run indoors and wish they had brought an umbrella. After an hour, the rain stops, and the sun shines again. That is how it goes, again and again.

This is not a joke. A sudden change in the weather can be dangerous. Although we cannot stop rain or snow, we can still pay attention to it. If it might snow, we wear warm clothes. If there is ice on the road, we walk slowly and do not drive fast. If a storm is coming, we stay away from windows and wait until it is over.

Weather is never boring for the people who live here. It is a little bit of everything: hot and cold, wet and dry, fine and terrible. You never know what it will be like tomorrow.
