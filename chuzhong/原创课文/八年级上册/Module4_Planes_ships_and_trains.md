# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 4 Planes, ships and trains

> 主题：交通出行（最高级）；词汇对齐：road / accident / except / choice / classmate / far from / close / crowded / all the time / journey / book / park / outside / however / cost

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** How did you get to school today, Sam?

**Sam:** I took the underground. It is the fastest way, and it is not crowded at seven o'clock.

**Mia:** But it costs a lot. I think the bus is cheaper.

**Sam:** The bus is too slow. It stops all the time, and sometimes there is an accident on the road.

**Mia:** That is true. However, I live far from school, so the bus is the only choice without my parents' car.

**Sam:** What about the train? It is the most comfortable way to travel.

**Mia:** I agree. Last month I took the train to visit my uncle, and I booked my ticket on my phone.

**Sam:** Nice. When I grow up, I want to travel by plane, the fastest way of all.

## 短文（Reading）

### How Do We Travel?

People travel in different ways. We walk, ride bicycles, take buses, drive cars, catch trains and fly in planes. Each way has its own good points and bad points.

The underground is usually the quickest way to move around a big city. It is fast, and it is never too crowded if you start early. However, it can cost a lot, and you have to wait for the next train.

The bus is cheaper. It stops near your home, and you can see the city outside the window. But it is often late, and it is crowded all the time because many people use it.

Trains are comfortable. You can stand up, walk around, and enjoy a long journey. They are good for travelling between cities, but not every town has a train station.

Planes are the fastest, but they are not always close to the city centre. You have to travel to the airport, so a short trip does not always save time.

Many students in my class walk or take the bus to school. My classmate, however, lives far from school, so he takes the underground every morning. He says it is the best choice, because he never wants to be late.
