## 对话（Dialogue）

**Emma:** Did you read about the pandas last night?

**Leo:** Yes. They are in danger, aren't they?

**Emma:** Right. There are only a few thousand left in the wild, so scientists are worried about the situation.

**Leo:** What are they doing to protect them?

**Emma:** They set up nature parks in the southwest of China. The pandas can live and feed in peace there.

**Leo:** That sounds good. Do they look after the babies?

**Emma:** Yes. Scientists raise the baby pandas and take away the danger. In order to help them, the government gives enough land to the parks.

**Leo:** I'm glad people are interested in saving them. Pandas are a symbol of China.

## 短文（Reading）

### Saving the Pandas

The giant panda is a symbol of China and a favourite of people around the world. Sadly, it is also in danger. In the past, there were many pandas in the wild, but now there are only a few thousand. Scientists say the main problem is that pandas do not have enough bamboo to eat.

Pandas live in the mountains of the southwest of China. As towns grow, the bamboo forests become smaller, and the wild animals have less food and less space. A panda baby is very small, and it cannot look after itself. In the wild, many babies do not grow up because they are cold or hungry.

To help the pandas, the government has set up nature parks and reserves. Scientists work there all year. They research the pandas, feed them, and raise the babies until they are strong. They also plant bamboo, so the pandas have enough food to live in peace.

After years of hard work, the number of pandas is slowly growing. This gives scientists hope. They believe that if we protect nature and wild animals, the pandas will always be with us.
