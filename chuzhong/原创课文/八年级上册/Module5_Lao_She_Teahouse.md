# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 5 Lao She Teahouse

> 主题：老舍茶馆与中国文化；词汇对齐：actress / teahouse / offer / end / in the end / no idea / act / show / common / twentieth / describe / society / head teacher / college / novel / name / if / magic

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Have you ever been to a teahouse in China?

**Lily:** Yes, I went to a famous teahouse in Beijing with my parents. The show was about the changes in Chinese society.

**Tom:** That sounds interesting. Do you remember the name of the play?

**Lily:** No idea! But I know that an old writer named Lao She wrote it. He is very famous.

**Tom:** Did you like the actors?

**Lily:** Yes, the actress who played the main character was great. At the end, everybody clapped.

**Tom:** I'd like to go there too. Can you take me one day?

**Lily:** Of course. I'll book the tickets and we can watch a tea show together.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Cup of Tea and a Story

In the centre of Beijing there is an old teahouse with a warm, common name. Every evening, visitors come in from the street, sit at round tables, and wait for the show to begin.

The play is named after a great writer. Over a hundred years ago, he wrote about a family, a teahouse and the changes in Chinese society. The show is not a long novel, but it tells a big story. It describes how people lived and how times changed.

On the stage, the actors move and talk while the audience drinks tea. The main actress plays a kind woman, and her performance makes the whole show feel like magic. Some who watch it for the first time have no idea how the story will end. In the end, many of them feel sad, but everyone claps.

Today, the teahouse is a common place for students and travellers. If you visit Beijing, you should not miss it. It is a wonderful way to understand Chinese culture, and a chance to enjoy a cup of tea and a story at the same time.
