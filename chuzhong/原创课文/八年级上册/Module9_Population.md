# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 9 Population

> 主题：人口问题；词汇对齐：noise / prepare / report / billion / huge / increase / hang on / notes / cause / problem / grow / birth / flat / rubbish / quiet / local / close down / mention / pollution / solve / public / service / pupil / reduce / create

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Teacher:** We are going to write a report about our town. What problems does it have?

**Anna:** The population is growing very fast. More and more people are moving in, so there is a huge increase in traffic and noise.

**Leo:** That's true. Lots of new flats are being built, but our streets are still crowded.

**Teacher:** What about the environment?

**Anna:** There is too much rubbish, and the pollution from cars is getting worse. It is a serious problem.

**Leo:** I think the local government should create more public services and schools.

**Teacher:** Good idea. When you write your report, remember to take notes, and mention some ways to reduce the problem.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Growing Town

Our town is not big, but it is growing quickly. Twenty years ago, only a few thousand people lived here. Today, the population is over three hundred thousand, and the number is still increasing every year.

This growth brings both good things and bad things. On the one hand, new schools, shops and public services are being built, so there are more jobs. On the other hand, life is becoming noisier and more crowded. The streets are full of cars, and the problem of pollution is getting worse. Large buildings and new flats take the place of quiet fields and gardens.

Some local people are worried. They say that too much traffic and rubbish cause health problems. They also want the government to build more homes before the town gets too crowded.

How can we solve these problems? First, we should reduce the rubbish we make and reuse what we use. Second, we can walk or take the bus instead of driving a car. Third, the government must plan for the future before the population grows too large.

A town is like a family. It grows, and it must look after itself. If people and the government work together, our town will be a good place to live.
