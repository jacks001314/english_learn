# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 2 My home town and my country

> 主题：家乡与国家（形容词比较级）；词汇对齐：hill / population / wide / million / north / south / west / especially / be famous for / university / island / area / low / mountain / countryside / umbrella / pretty good

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Anna:** Where do you come from, Mark?

**Mark:** I come from a small town in the north of England. It is much smaller than this city.

**Anna:** Really? What is it like?

**Mark:** It is very quiet and pretty. There are low hills and a wide river, and the countryside is green all year round.

**Anna:** Is it famous for anything?

**Mark:** Yes, it is famous for its old university. Many visitors come here, especially in summer.

**Anna:** What about the population?

**Mark:** Only about two hundred thousand people. It is not as crowded as a big city.

**Anna:** It sounds like a nice home town. I would like to visit it one day.

## 短文（Reading）

### My Home Town

My home town is a small town in the north of my country. It is a quiet place with a population of about two hundred thousand. You will not find any tall buildings here. Instead, you will see low houses, green hills and a wide river running through the town.

The town is famous for its old university. Students come from all over the country to study here, especially in autumn when the leaves turn red. In summer, visitors sit by the river and watch the boats, and they often say that the town is very pretty.

We also have some beautiful mountains and an island near the coast, but the countryside is my favourite part. It is quiet and clean, and the air is fresh.

People sometimes ask me why I do not want to move to a bigger city. I always give the same answer: a big city is exciting, but my home town is where my heart is. It may be small, but it is pretty good.
