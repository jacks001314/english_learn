# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 7 A famous story

> 主题：著名故事（过去进行时）；词汇对齐：fall / follow / hole / rabbit / ssh / ground / tea party / twice / once or twice / suddenly / pink / pocket / field / think about / deep / while / land / dry

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mum:** What are you reading, Lucy?

**Lucy:** A famous story about a girl and a rabbit. While she was sitting in a garden, she suddenly saw a rabbit running past her.

**Mum:** Did the rabbit speak?

**Lucy:** Yes! The rabbit was looking at a small watch and saying, "Oh dear, I'm late!" The girl was so surprised that she followed it into a deep hole.

**Mum:** And what happened next?

**Lucy:** She fell and landed on the ground, and then she came to a strange tea party. People were sitting around a table while she was wondering where she was.

**Mum:** What a wonderful story! I think I have read it once or twice myself.

## 短文（Reading）

### Down the Rabbit Hole

One hot afternoon, a girl was sitting quietly in a garden with her sister. She was feeling bored, and she did not know what to do. Then she saw a white rabbit running past her. It was not a common rabbit, because it was wearing a coat and carrying a watch.

The rabbit stopped for a moment, took the watch out of its pocket, and looked at it. "Oh dear, oh dear, I'm late!" it cried. Before the girl could think about it, the rabbit jumped into a small hole under a tree.

The girl was very interested, so she followed it. She ran, and then she began to fall down, down, down into a very deep hole. The hole was so deep that she thought she was falling all the way to the other side of the world.

At the bottom, she landed softly on the ground. Around her, the dry earth had turned into a strange, beautiful land. A small door stood in the wall, and she could hear the sound of a tea party coming from behind it.

The girl did not know where she was, but she was not afraid. She was still thinking about the rabbit, and she wanted to find it. And that is how her wonderful adventure began.
