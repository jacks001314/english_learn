## 对话（Dialogue）

**May:** Ben, are you all right? What happened?

**Ben:** I fell down the stairs and I think my leg is broken. It really hurts.

**May:** Don't move. Let me help you. First, we should call for medical help.

**Ben:** My phone is at the bottom of my bag. I can't reach it.

**May:** It's OK. I'll call the doctor and stay with you. Keep calm.

**Ben:** What should I do while we wait?

**May:** Just stay still and keep clear of the edge. Lift your head a little if you can.

**Ben:** That helps. Thank you, May. You are so helpful.

## 短文（Reading）

### Help Comes First

Accidents can happen at any time. When they do, knowing what to do is very important. The most useful thing we can learn, after the habit of being careful, is first aid.

If someone falls and breaks an arm, do not move the person. Instead, stay calm and call for medical help. Make sure the area is safe, and keep people clear of the injured person. If there is blood, cover the wound with a clean cloth and press it gently. This can reduce the pain until a doctor arrives.

In an earthquake, the rules are different. First, stay inside and get under a strong table. Keep away from windows, because broken glass can be harmful. If the ground is shaking, drop, cover and hold on. After the shaking stops, leave the building carefully and do not use the stairs; use the exit steps beside the building, or a safe door.

We cannot always stop accidents from happening. But we can prepare ourselves for them. Schools teach students about danger, and some towns even warn people by giving them training on how to react. When we keep these simple rules in our minds, we can be brave and helpful when it matters most. That is the true power of help.
