## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lucy:** I'm having dinner with a British family tonight. What should I do?

**Ben:** You must be polite and wait for them to start. Also, when you get a gift, you should open it in front of them.

**Lucy:** Really? In my country, we usually open a gift later.

**Ben:** That's a small difference. Here, people use a knife and fork, and they often have fish and chips.

**Lucy:** Do I have to use chopsticks?

**Ben:** No, for Western food you use a knife and fork. It is a good experience for you.

**Lucy:** Thanks. It's my first time, so I'll try to remember your advice.

## 短文（Reading）

### At the Dinner Table

Different countries have different ways of life. Some of them are small, but they can be very serious traditions. For people who visit a new place for the first time, it is important to know what to do.

In Britain, for example, people usually have a cup of tea with milk, and a west-country dinner often includes fish and chips. At the table, a gentleman may hold the door open for others, and he often helps the person next to him. When guests arrive, a host may offer them a small gift, such as a toy or a video game for a child.

When you receive a gift, you should accept it with a smile and open it immediately. In some cultures, the words "thank you" are enough. In others, you must say something about the present, or it may seem that you do not like it.

At the dinner table, there are also rules. You should not start eating before the host. You should keep your hands above the table, and if you need something, you should ask someone to pass it to you. An important rule is simple: be polite, and enjoy the experience of trying new food and new ways.

People say that the best way to understand a country is to sit down and eat with its people. When you taste their food, you taste their way of life.
