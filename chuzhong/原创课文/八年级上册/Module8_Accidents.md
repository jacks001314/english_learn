# 原创课文 · 八年级上册 Module 8 Accidents

> 主题：事故与急救；词汇对齐：pale / appear / round / corner / hit / glad / in time / fall off / risk / attention / pay attention / side / side by side / bite / climb / hide / throw / fridge / pain / worse / medicine

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Doctor:** Come in and sit down. What's the matter?

**Tom:** I fell off my bike on my way to school. My knee hurts and the skin is broken.

**Doctor:** When did it happen?

**Tom:** About half an hour ago. A car appeared suddenly at the corner, and I had to stop. I was lucky that it didn't hit me, but I fell to the side of the road.

**Doctor:** Does it hurt a lot?

**Tom:** Yes, and it's getting worse.

**Doctor:** Let me look. Don't worry, it's not too bad. You need to keep the wound clean and take some medicine. Please pay attention to the traffic next time.

**Tom:** I will. Thank you, doctor.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Lucky Day

Last Monday was a cold day. Ben was riding his bike to school, thinking about the maths test. He was not paying attention to the road, so he did not see the car coming around the corner.

The driver shouted, and the car stopped just in time. But Ben was so surprised that he fell off his bike and hit the ground. His face turned pale, and his arm was in pain.

A kind woman came to help him. She took Ben's bag, and together they walked to the side of the road. "Are you all right?" she asked. "You must pay attention to the traffic. Cars can appear very suddenly."

Ben thanked her and checked his arm. The skin was hurt but not broken. He was glad that nothing worse had happened.

That evening, his mother cleaned the cut and gave him some medicine. She told him to be more careful the next time.

Now, whenever Ben rides his bike, he always looks round before he turns. He knows that a small mistake can lead to a big accident, and that safety comes first.
