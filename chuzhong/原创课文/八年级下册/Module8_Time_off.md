# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 8 Time off

> 主题：度假与休闲；词汇对齐：hardly / take up / point out / sights / thirsty / at the top of / waste / square / kilometre / shape / human / wake / wake sb. up / somebody / about / path / pull / freshwater / woken

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Anna:** What do you like to do when you have time off, Tom?

**Tom:** I go to the mountains. There are so many wonderful sights up there.

**Anna:** Is it hard to climb?

**Tom:** A little. The path is long, and sometimes I am thirsty and tired. But when I reach the top, the view is amazing.

**Anna:** Can you see the whole town from there?

**Tom:** Yes. The lake below is a perfect shape, and the square is as small as a stamp.

**Anna:** That sounds lovely. I waste too much time sitting at home. Maybe I should get up and go outside too.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Day in the Mountains

My family loves the mountains. Whenever we have time off, we put on our shoes, take a little water, and walk along the path into the hills.

Last Saturday, we set out early. The morning air was cool and fresh, and the birds were singing. We climbed for hours. The path was steep, and I was hot and thirsty. "I hardly have any strength left," I said. "Can we rest?"

My father pointed at the top. "Not yet," he said. "The best sights are at the top of the mountain."

So we kept going. Along the way, we saw a small stream of clear freshwater. We stopped and drank, and the water was cold and sweet. We also saw a little animal that pulled something across the path. It was a beautiful day to be outside.

At last, we reached the top. The town below looked like a small toy. The lake was a big, round shape, and the square was as small as a coin. For a moment, I forgot all my worries. I stood there, feeling tiny but very happy.

Somebody once said that we should wake up each day and enjoy the world. I now understand what that means. When we are busy, we forget to look around. But when we take time off, we remember how beautiful the earth is.

On the way home, I felt tired but happy. The day was not wasted at all.
