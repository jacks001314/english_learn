# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 3 Journey to space

> 主题：太空探索（现在完成时）；词汇对齐：earth / moon / news / planet / reach / yet / just / model / spaceship / project / no problem / latest / discover / astronaut / space travel / none / environment / solar / system / group / galaxy / universe / light / impossible

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Have you heard the latest news? A new spaceship has just reached the Moon.

**Mia:** Really? I have read about space travel before, but I have never seen a real spacecraft.

**Tom:** Our science club is building a model of a rocket. Would you like to join?

**Mia:** Yes! I have always wanted to learn about the solar system.

**Tom:** Great. We will look at the planets and the stars in the galaxy.

**Mia:** It sounds like a wonderful project. Maybe one day we will even find life on another planet.

## 短文（Reading）

### New Worlds

For thousands of years, people have looked up at the night sky and wondered what is out there. Today, that wonder has become a journey into space.

The first rockets carried only animals and simple instruments. Then, in 1961, a human went into space for the first time. It was a moment that changed history. Since then, astronauts have travelled further and further. They have walked on the Moon, sent machines to other planets, and built space stations that circle the Earth.

Space is very different from our home. There is no air, and the temperature can be very hot or very cold. Because of this, astronauts have to wear special suits and carry their own oxygen. They have to learn to work, eat and sleep in a tiny space. It is not an easy life, but it is an exciting one.

Our project at school is about the solar system. We have made a small model of the eight planets, and we have placed a tiny sun in the middle. We have learned that the Earth is one of eight planets, and that the Moon travels around it. We have also learned about the galaxy we live in, and about the universe of stars and planets beyond it.

We have not discovered life on other planets yet. Some scientists believe it is possible, while others think it is impossible. On this question, the journey is not over. Every new spaceship, every new telescope and every new photograph brings us a little closer to the answer.

Maybe one day, one of us will travel to the stars.
