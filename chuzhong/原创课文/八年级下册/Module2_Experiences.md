# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 2 Experiences

> 主题：经历（现在完成时）；词汇对齐：ever / enter / competition / prize / first prize / dream / afford / write about / make up / invite / move / send / Germany / France / tower / ancient / king / queen / Arabic / way / mix / miss / count / count down

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Emma:** Have you ever entered a writing competition?

**Leo:** Yes, I have. Last year I entered one and won the first prize!

**Emma:** That's amazing! What did you write about?

**Leo:** I wrote about my dream of visiting France. I described the ancient towers and how I would mix with the local people.

**Emma:** Did they give you a prize?

**Leo:** They sent me a book and an invitation to a writing club. I couldn't afford to travel, but the prize was still great.

**Emma:** I have never entered one. Maybe I should try.

**Leo:** You should. Everyone has a story to tell.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Dream Comes True

Have you ever dreamed of seeing the world? Many of us have. Some dreams stay in our hearts for years, and then, unexpectedly, they come true.

My friend Lena always loved stories about other countries. When she was a child, she used to read about France and Germany, and she imagined herself walking through the streets of Paris. She wished she could visit the Eiffel Tower or stand in front of an ancient castle. But money was short, and her family could not afford such a long trip.

Then one day, her school announced a writing competition. The topic was "My Dream Journey." Lena was excited. She wrote about a trip she had never taken: walking by a river, speaking a little Arabic to a shopkeeper, and watching the sun go down behind an old tower. She made up many of the details, but she wrote every word with her heart.

To her surprise, Lena won the first prize. The prize was a trip to France, together with a group of students. She could not believe her eyes. After years of dreaming, she was finally going.

In France, Lena saw everything she had written about. It did not feel like a dream. It felt like coming home. Since that trip, she has written three more stories, and she has started a small blog to share her experiences.

Lena says that we should never stop dreaming. A dream may seem impossible, but if we work for it, it can come true. You have not missed your chance yet. Count down the days, and start today.
