# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 4 Seeing the doctor

> 主题：健康与就医（现在完成时 + since/for）；词汇对齐：cough / fever / headache / ache / toothache / ill / since / catch a cold / take sb.'s temperature / fast food / health / well / heart / active / pet / member / take part in / condition / in excellent condition / sleepy / daily / weak

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Doctor:** Good morning. What's the matter?

**Ben:** I have a cough and a headache. I've felt ill since Monday.

**Doctor:** Let me take your temperature. ... You have a fever. Have you caught a cold?

**Ben:** I think so. It was raining, and I stayed outside for a long time.

**Doctor:** You haven't been feeling well. Have you eaten fast food a lot?

**Ben:** Yes, I have. I eat it almost every day.

**Doctor:** That's not healthy. You should eat well and take part in sports.

**Ben:** I will, doctor. Thank you.

## 短文（Reading）

### Look After Yourself

Health is the most important thing we have. We often do not think about it until we become ill, and then it is too late. A doctor can help us when we are sick, but the best medicine is a good habit.

Many students these days do not move enough. They sit at their desks for hours, eat fast food, and go to bed very late. Soon they catch a cold easily, or they get a headache and a cough. Their body becomes weak, and they feel sleepy all day.

The good news is that a healthy life is not difficult. First, eat well. Eat vegetables and fruit, and drink enough water. Do not take too much sugar or too much salt. Second, be active. Take part in sports or go for a walk every day. This makes your heart strong and keeps you in excellent condition. Third, sleep enough. Your body needs rest to repair itself, especially after a long day of school.

Doctors also say that our feelings are a part of our health. When we are worried or sad, we may feel ill. So it is important to talk to someone and to enjoy the simple things in life. A pet or a friend can be a great help.

If you have a fever or a toothache, do not wait. See a doctor early, and take the medicine you are given. But remember: the best way to stay well is to look after yourself every day.
