# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 9 Friendship

> 主题：友谊（宾语从句）；词汇对齐：helpline / separate / explain / refuse / treat / herself / whether / lonely / regret / introduce / join in / no problem / silence / in silence / pass / bright / treasure / day by day / trust / include / circle / stick / glue / suggestion / patient

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Emma:** You look quiet. Is something wrong?

**Anna:** I had a fight with my best friend. I regret what I said to her.

**Emma:** It is hard to lose a friend. Have you tried to explain your feelings?

**Anna:** I did, but she refused to talk. I feel so lonely now.

**Emma:** Give her a little time. Trust that the friendship can be mended. A good suggestion is to write her a note.

**Anna:** You are right. I will try to talk to her again tomorrow.

**Emma:** Be patient, and things will get better day by day.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Friend Worth Keeping

True friendship is one of the most beautiful treasures in life. A good friend is with us in the bright days and in the dark ones. But friendship is not always easy. Sometimes there are misunderstandings, and sometimes we hurt the people we love.

My friend Lucy and I have known each other since we were little. We used to do everything together. Then one day, we had a small fight over a project. I wanted to do it my way, and she wanted to do it hers. We both said things we did not mean, and then we stopped talking.

For a few days, we kept our silence. It was a sad time. I felt lonely and I did not know what to do. I told my mother about it, and she gave me some advice. She said, "Do not let a small thing separate you. You should explain how you feel and listen to her, too."

So I did as she suggested. I wrote a note to Lucy, and I passed it to her after class. I wrote that I was sorry and that I valued our friendship. She read it, and she smiled at me. That afternoon, we walked home together and talked for a long time.

That day I learned that friendship needs trust and patience. We have to be kind, even when we are angry. And we have to build the bond again, day by day, like glue that holds two pieces together.

Lucy is still my best friend. Our friendship has survived, and it is stronger now. I know that whatever happens, we will find a way to be friends again.
