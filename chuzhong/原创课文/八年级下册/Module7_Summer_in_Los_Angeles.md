# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 7 Summer in Los Angeles

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Have you decided to join the summer camp in LA?

**Lucy:** Yes, I have. My parents have already paid for it.

**Tom:** What do you need to prepare?

**Lucy:** I need my passport, and I have made a list of things to take, like sunglasses and shorts.

**Tom:** Will you stay in a hotel?

**Lucy:** No, I will have a homestay with an American family. It is a good way to learn about their daily life.

**Tom:** That sounds great. I prefer home life, but it is a wonderful chance to practise my English.

**Lucy:** It is. In the end, I hope to make lifelong friends.

## 短文（Reading）

### My Summer in Los Angeles

This summer, I am going to Los Angeles with a group of students for a language camp. I have never been abroad before, so I am both excited and a little nervous.

Before I go, I have a lot to do. First, I need to get my passport ready. I have also made a list of everything I need: sunglasses for the sun, shorts and trousers, a pair of good shoes, and a small camera. My suitcase has become quite heavy, so I will have to weigh it at the airport. I cannot take too much, because there is a total weight limit.

The best part of the trip is the homestay. I will live with an American family, and in this way, I can learn about their daily life. In the morning, I will have breakfast with them, and at night, I will tell them about my day. They will provide me with a warm room and good food, and I will help them a little at the same time. This is a wonderful way to build true friendship.

During the day, we will take English classes and do fun activities. We will visit interesting places, take a trip to the sea, and work in small groups. At the end of the course, there will be a test, but I am not worried. The most important thing is to speak and to enjoy myself.

Some of my friends think I am a little crazy to go so far away. But I know that this month will last in my memory for a lifetime. I cannot wait to make new friends and to see the world. This trip depends on nothing but my own courage, and I am ready for the adventure.
