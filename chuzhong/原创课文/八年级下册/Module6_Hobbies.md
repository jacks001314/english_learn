# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 6 Hobbies

> 主题：兴趣爱好；词汇对齐：fan / stamp / tidy up / shelf / have a look / coin / note / pound / dollar / must / valuable / value / person / interest / skill / activity / sailing / come out / result / as a result / pleasure / success / encourage

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What do you do in your free time, Leo?

**Leo:** I collect stamps. My grandfather started it, and it has become my interest too.

**Mia:** Do they have much value?

**Leo:** Some are quite valuable, but for me, the pleasure is in the collection itself.

**Mia:** Have a look at my shelf - I have some old coins and notes from different places.

**Leo:** Wow! That's a great collection. Hobbies are useful, because they teach us new skills.

**Mia:** And they help us enjoy life. As a result, we become more positive people.

## 短文（Reading）

### Hobbies Make Us Grow

Everybody has a hobby. It can be a sport, a game, a collection or a craft. Some people like to sail, some love to play music, and some enjoy making things with their hands.

A hobby is more than a way to pass the time. It teaches us useful skills and can even lead to success. My cousin Tom started to collect stamps when he was six. He learned to read about different countries, to be careful with his things, and to keep his collection tidy. He was always happy to tidy up the shelf and show visitors his latest pieces.

Over the years, Tom's collection grew. Some stamps were not worth much, but a few were very valuable. He once found a rare stamp at a small shop for only a few pounds. He took a photo and posted it online. The news came out, and many collectors wanted to buy it. Tom chose to keep it, because it was part of his story.

As a result, Tom became a kind of expert. People asked for his advice, and he started to write a small blog about stamps. He encouraged other students to start their own collections. In this way, a simple hobby opened many doors for him.

Of course, we should not let a hobby take up all our time. School is important, and a hobby is a part of life, not the whole of it.

A hobby is like a friend. It entertains us, it teaches us, and it gives us pleasure and confidence. Whatever you love to do, keep doing it. It may become something great.
