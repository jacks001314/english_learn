# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 1 Feelings and impressions

> 主题：感觉与印象（系动词）；词汇对齐：smell / soft / sour / pizza / lovely / done / try / Have a try / pie / sound / sweet tooth / salt / jam / favourite / ear / glasses / jeans / fair / pretty / proud / be proud of / stranger / message / can't wait

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Amy:** This soup smells delicious. Did you make it yourself?

**Tom:** Yes. I tried a new recipe. Would you like to have a try?

**Amy:** Sure! ... Mmm, it tastes lovely. Is there sugar in it?

**Tom:** A little. I have a sweet tooth, so I added some jam.

**Amy:** It looks a little soft, but it's really good. What about this pie?

**Tom:** That one is still in the oven. Wait until dinner!

**Amy:** I can't wait. You are great at cooking, Tom. I'm proud of you.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Friend's First Visit

My pen friend Ella is coming to visit me for the first time. I am a little nervous, because I want her to have a good impression of my town and my family.

This morning, I went to the station to meet her. The moment I saw her, I knew it was Ella. She had fair hair and wore round glasses and blue jeans. She looked friendly and a little shy.

I took her home. The whole kitchen was full of a sweet smell, because my mother was making an apple pie. Ella said she loved it, and she asked for the recipe right away. She has a sweet tooth, just like me.

At dinner, we talked about many things. Ella told me about her school, and I told her about mine. She was a little nervous about meeting my parents, but they were very kind, and soon she felt at home.

The best moment came after dinner. My father played a song, and Ella began to sing along softly. It sounded wonderful, and everybody smiled. In that moment, I knew we would be good friends for a long time.

First impressions are important, but they are only the beginning. The real friendship shows itself little by little, in the small, warm moments we share.
