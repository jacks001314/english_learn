# 原创课文 · 八年级下册 Module 10 On the radio

> 主题：广播节目与梦想；词汇对齐：director / show / show sb. around / on air / avoid / background / national / international / presenter / interview / seem / listener / in person / part-time / article / studio / purpose

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What do you want to be when you grow up, Leo?

**Leo:** I want to be a radio presenter. I love talking to people.

**Mia:** That is a great dream. How did you become interested in the radio?

**Leo:** I once visited a studio with my father. I watched a presenter interviewing a singer live on air.

**Mia:** Did you meet the director?

**Leo:** Yes. He showed us around the studio, and he explained how the show is made.

**Mia:** It sounds exciting. I hope you can make your dream come true.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Voice on the Radio

My dream began a long time ago, when I was a small boy. Every morning, my father listened to the radio while he made breakfast. He said that a good voice could travel all over the country, and that words could reach people's hearts.

One day, my father took me to visit a radio station. A friendly presenter gave us a tour. He showed us around the studio, where the microphones stood ready. He also explained how to read the news, how to talk to a guest, and how to avoid making mistakes when the show was on air.

That day I learned something important. A radio show is not just a man talking to a machine. Behind every voice there is a team. There is a director, who plans the show. There are writers, who make the script. There are technicians, who control the background music and the sound.

In the studio, I watched a presenter interview a special guest. They were calm and warm, and they treated each other with respect. The camera and the microphone seemed to disappear. It was just two people talking.

Later, I found out that the presenter also worked part-time as a writer for a national newspaper. In the evening, he wrote articles, and in the morning, he spoke to thousands of listeners. He told me that the purpose of the radio was to connect people, and to bring a little light into their lives.

Now I know what I want to do. I want to be a voice for people, to share good stories, and to bring people together. Whether it is a small school show or an international station, I will keep working for my dream.
