## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lucy:** What are you watching, Tom?

**Tom:** A cartoon about a brave cat. He fights the bad guys and always saves the day.

**Lucy:** Is he cool?

**Tom:** Yes! He is smart and humorous, and he has a strong heart. He wins the hearts of everyone.

**Lucy:** I like cartoons, too. They are funny, but they also teach us lessons.

**Tom:** Right. A good cartoon can make us laugh and think at the same time.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Little Hero

Everyone loves a good story, and cartoons are stories in pictures. They are funny, colourful and full of action. But a really good cartoon is much more than a child's game.

Think about a little cat who is not big or strong. He is a little ugly, and his home is a small corner under the roof. But he has a smart mind and a kind heart. Every day, he helps the people around him. He saves a lost child, he leads his friends out of a mess, and he fights the trouble that comes to the house.

People love this cat because he is not perfect. He is like us. He makes mistakes, and sometimes he is afraid. But when something is wrong, he stands up and tries. He is humorous, too. Even in a dangerous moment, he finds something to laugh about. That is why he has won the hearts of so many people.

Some cartoons have been with us for a very long time. They appear again and again, in books, in films, even on schoolbags. An artist once invented a character, and then copied the same idea for years. Some stories are shown in black-and-white, but the feelings are still bright.

Cartoons are a gift. They give us a few minutes of joy and a memory that stays with us. They may be simple, but they carry a big lesson: be brave, be kind, and never give up.
