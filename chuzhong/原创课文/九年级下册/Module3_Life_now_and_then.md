# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 3 Life now and then

> 主题：现在与过去的生活（形容词与副词）；词汇对齐：wealthy / fear / used to / wealth / double / seldom / spare / spare time / speak up / deaf / tiny / electric / light / candle / postman / cold / heat / full-time / role / education / transport

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Grandma:** Life was very different when I was young, Tom.

**Tom:** Really? What was it like?

**Grandma:** We had no electric lights, so we used candles at night. The houses were small and the cold in winter was terrible.

**Tom:** Did you have many things to do?

**Grandma:** No, we seldom had spare time. We worked in the fields all day. But we had a lot of fun together.

**Tom:** I think life is easier now, but maybe you were happier then.

**Grandma:** Maybe. Money does not always bring happiness.

## 短文（Reading）

### Life Now and Then

My grandmother is a kind old lady. She often tells me stories about her life when she was a girl, and they make me think about how much the world has changed.

When my grandmother was young, life was not easy. Her family was poor, and they were not wealthy. The house was tiny, and there were several children in one small room. In winter, the cold was very hard, and there was little heat. At night, they used candles instead of electric lights.

There was also no television and no computer. Children seldom had spare time, because they had to work in the fields and help the family. My grandmother had to walk a long way to school. There was no bus, and the transport was by horse or on foot.

But my grandmother says that life was happy in another way. People knew each other well, and they helped one another. When a postman came to the village, he would sit and talk for a while. In the evenings, families would sit together and tell stories by the fire.

Today, life is much easier. We have warm homes, good food, and cars to take us anywhere. We have electric light, so we can read and work at night. We have schools and hospitals, and a full-time education is open to everyone.

Of course, life is faster now, and people often have no time to stop and talk. We fear we may lose the warmth of the past.

I think both times have their good points. We should be thankful for the easy life we have today, but we should also keep the kindness of the old days. Technology cannot replace a good heart.
