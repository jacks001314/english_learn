# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 4 Rules and suggestions

> 主题：规则与建议（情态动词）；词汇对齐：set off / sock / whenever / proper / edge / yourself / go off / starve / in one go / rock / rock climbing / stone / fairly / smooth / straight / tent / fall / fall asleep / hang / sudden / gun / soft / still / wood / blood

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Ben:** We are going to go rock climbing this weekend. Would you like to come?

**Tom:** I would, but I am a little worried. Is it safe?

**Ben:** It is, as long as we follow the rules. We must set off early and wear proper shoes and a strong rope.

**Tom:** What should I bring?

**Ben:** Bring food, a bottle of water, and a warm jacket. Do not get too close to the edge.

**Tom:** OK. I will listen carefully and do as you say.

## 短文（Reading）

### Up the Cliff

Last autumn, my class went on a school trip to climb a big cliff. It was one of the most exciting days of my life, and it also taught me some very important rules.

We set off early in the morning. The sky was clear, and the air was cool. Our teacher gave each of us a few simple suggestions: wear proper shoes, carry enough water, and do not go too near the edge. She told us to walk slowly and to hold the rope carefully.

The climb was hard but beautiful. The rocks were rough but smooth in some places. We climbed in small steps, and we did not try to do it all in one go. When we were tired, we stopped and rested. We sat on the white stones, ate our lunch, and watched the birds fly far below us.

At one point, we came to a very narrow part. The path was straight and the ground was steep. I was a little afraid, but I remembered to stay calm and keep my balance. Step by step, I moved along the edge of the rock.

At the top, the view was amazing. We could see the whole valley, the river, and the small houses of our town far away. I felt so proud.

On the way back, we were very tired, and some of us almost fell asleep on the bus. But we were all happy.

The trip taught me that rules are not made to stop us from having fun. They are there to keep us safe, so that we can enjoy the adventure without fear. Whenever we go into the mountains, I remember these simple words.
