# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 2 Education

> 主题：教育（代词）；词汇对齐：ours / tie / row / pool / pass / secondary / secondary school / absent / bell

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What is your school like, Leo?

**Leo:** It is a big secondary school. We sit in rows in a bright classroom, and we wear a tie.

**Mia:** Do you have a swimming pool?

**Leo:** No, but we have a big sports field. We swim at the city pool after school.

**Mia:** I like our old school bell. It rings at the start of every class.

**Leo:** That is nice. Little things like that make a school feel like a second home.

## 短文（Reading）

### My School, My Home

Some people say that school is a second home, and I agree with them. Our school is not the biggest or the most modern, but it is a special place where we learn, grow and make friends.

My school is a secondary school, and it sits on a quiet street. When you walk in through the gate, you see a red building with long windows. In the morning, the bright sun shines on the glass, and our school looks warm and friendly.

In the classroom, we sit in rows facing the blackboard. Every student has a desk and a chair. We wear a uniform, and we tie a blue tie around our necks. The teacher stands at the front and explains, while we listen and take notes. At ten o'clock, the old bell rings, and we run out into the yard for a short rest.

Our school has good teachers who care about us. They help us with our homework and listen to our problems. They are patient and kind, and they always encourage us to try our best.

We also have a small library and a sports field. In the afternoon, we play football or basketball. Some days, we swim at the pool near the school. I am never absent from these lessons, because I love them.

Looking back, I have learned many things here. I have learned English, maths and science. But more importantly, I have learned how to be a good person. The friendships we make and the lessons we learn together will stay with us for the rest of our lives.

Education is not only about books. It is about learning to see the world, to think for ourselves, and to help one another. That is what makes a school so important to us.
