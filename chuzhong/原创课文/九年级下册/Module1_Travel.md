# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 1 Travel

> 主题：旅行（名词、冠词、数词）；词汇对齐：flight / because of / direct / pilot / succeed / as long as / school-leaver / exactly / take care / sir / officer / stupid / take off / jacket

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Where are you going for your holiday, Lily?

**Lily:** I am going to take a flight to a small country in Europe.

**Tom:** Is it a direct flight?

**Lily:** Yes, it is. It takes about seven hours, so I will be a little tired. But I cannot wait to see the old streets.

**Tom:** I hope you succeed in finding your dream place.

**Lily:** Thank you. As long as the weather is fine, everything will be wonderful.

**Tom:** Take care, and send me a postcard!

## 短文（Reading）

### The Long Flight

Last summer, I took my first long flight. I was very excited, but also a little nervous. I had never travelled so far before, and I did not know what to expect.

At the airport, a friendly officer checked my ticket and my passport. "Where are you going?" he asked. I told him exactly where I was going. "Have a good trip," he said with a smile. I felt much better because of his kind words.

The plane took off right on time. As we rose into the sky, my city became smaller and smaller. The houses looked like little toys, and the roads looked like thin lines. I watched the clouds below and felt very high up.

It was a long flight, and I was not as comfortable as I had hoped. My legs felt stiff, and the food was not very good. The pilot told us that we would have a little bad weather for a short time. But I did not mind. I was too busy looking out of the window.

After seven hours, the plane came down gently. When I stepped out, the air was warm and different. Everything about the new country was strange and exciting.

That trip taught me something important. Travel is not always easy, but it is always worth it. As long as we are brave and patient, we can see wonderful places and meet wonderful people.

Now, whenever I hear the sound of an engine, I think of the open sky and the wide world. A journey is a gift we give to ourselves, and I want to take many more.
