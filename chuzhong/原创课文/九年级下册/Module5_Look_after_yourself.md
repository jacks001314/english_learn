# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 5 Look after yourself

> 主题：自我照顾与健康；词汇对齐：catch up / blind / ouch / call off / thanks to / health care / expect / require / physical / effort / once in a while / harm / agreement

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Doctor:** How are you today, Ben?

**Ben:** I'm a little better, doctor. Thanks to this medicine, I have caught up on my sleep.

**Doctor:** Good. But you need to take care of yourself every day, not just when you are ill.

**Ben:** What should I do?

**Doctor:** Eat well, rest enough, and do some physical exercise. It is not a lot of effort.

**Ben:** I will. I used to work so hard that I forgot to sleep.

**Doctor:** That can harm your health. Remember, your body is your best friend. Take care of it.

## 短文（Reading）

### Look After Yourself

We often hear people say that health is the most important thing in life. But how many of us really take care of ourselves? We work hard, study late, and forget to rest. We eat fast food and sit in front of a screen all day. Then, one day, our body says, "Enough!"

My friend Ben learned this the hard way. He was a very hard-working student. He always wanted to be the best, and he spent every minute on his schoolwork. He would stay up late and get up early, and he seldom had time to eat well or to exercise.

One day, Ben felt very tired and his head hurt. He could not even see the words on the page clearly. He did not feel blind, but everything seemed to spin. His mother took him to the doctor. The doctor said that he was too tired, and that his body needed rest.

The doctor gave him a simple plan. He was expected to sleep eight hours a night and to eat healthy food. He was also asked to do some physical exercise, like walking or swimming, once in a while. It did not require a lot of effort, he said. It just needed to be done every day.

Ben followed the plan. After a few weeks, he felt much better. He could study well without feeling ill. He learned that working hard is good, but it should not harm our health.

We all need to agree on one thing: there is nothing more valuable than our health. We can get more money, more books and more things. But we cannot buy back our health once it is lost.

So take a little time to look after yourself. Eat well, move your body, rest your eyes and mind. It is the best gift you can give to yourself.
