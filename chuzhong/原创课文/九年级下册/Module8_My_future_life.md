# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 8 My future life

> 主题：未来生活与毕业；词汇对齐：handbag / beat / pardon / intend / fetch / pancake / rose / laugh at / kindness / give up / try one's best / disappointed / bedside / note / whom

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lily:** School is almost over. Do you have any plans for the future, Jack?

**Jack:** I intend to study at university and become an engineer.

**Lily:** That sounds great. What about you, Mia?

**Mia:** I want to be a doctor. I hope to help the sick and make people better.

**Jack:** We have all worked hard. Let's do our best and never give up on our dreams.

**Lily:** I agree. Even if we are disappointed sometimes, we must keep going.

## 短文（Reading）

### My Future Life

Graduation day is a special time. For the first time in our lives, we say goodbye to a place we have known for years. The school hall is full of flowers and a few red roses. Our teachers stand by the door, and some of them have tears in their eyes.

I remember the first day I came to this school. I was small and a little afraid. I carried a big schoolbag and a small handbag. At the gate, I did not know whom to talk to. Now, three years later, I am leaving with wonderful friends and memories.

Looking back, I think about all the little things. The times we laughed together, the lessons we learned, and the games we played. My best friend often made us pancakes for lunch, and we would laugh at our own silly stories. When I was sad, my friends were always there, and their kindness helped me a lot.

Now a new life is waiting for us. Some of my friends intend to go to a university. Others want to work, or to travel and see the world. I have a dream, too. I want to use what I have learned to help others, and I will never give up on it.

Of course, the future will not always be easy. There will be hard days, and we may sometimes feel disappointed. But we should remember the lessons of our school days: be brave, be kind, and try your best.

I will keep a little note by my bedside. It says these words: "Do not be afraid to try. You are stronger than you think." When I read it in the morning, I feel ready for a new day.

Goodbye, my school. Thank you for the years. Now, the future is calling, and I am ready to meet it.
