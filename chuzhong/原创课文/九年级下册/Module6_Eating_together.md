# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 6 Eating together

> 主题：聚餐与西方餐桌文化；词汇对齐：invitation / calendar / balloon / paint / heat / heat up / knife / fork / spoon / cheeseburger / Italian / westerner / west / serve / similar / wing / lady / gentleman / help yourself / cross

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** I got an invitation to a dinner party this weekend.

**Tom:** How nice! Is it a Western meal?

**Mia:** I think so. I am a little worried about the table manners.

**Tom:** Do not worry. Just use the knife and fork, and wait for the host to start.

**Mia:** What should I say?

**Tom:** Remember to say "thank you" and "help yourself" politely. A polite guest is always welcome.

## 短文（Reading）

### Dinner at a Western Table

Last month, I received an invitation to a dinner party at my friend Lily's house. It was my first time at a Western meal, so I was both excited and a little nervous.

On the day of the party, I marked the date on my calendar. I was careful not to be late. I even painted a small picture to give to Lily as a present.

When I arrived, the table was already set. There were plates, cups and glasses on both sides. In the middle, there was a big dish of food, and next to each plate were a knife, a fork and a spoon. Lily's mother served the food with a smile, and she said, "Help yourself!"

I chose some food and sat down. At first, I held my fork in the wrong hand, and I felt a little silly. But I watched the others and soon learned. In the West, people usually hold the fork in the left hand and the knife in the right. We eat together, and we talk at the same time.

The food was delicious. There were cheeses, roasted chicken and some golden, crisp wings. We also had some Italian pizza and cheeseburgers for everyone to share. The food was warm, and the house felt cosy.

At the table, I met many new people. There was a friendly lady, and a kind gentleman who told me funny stories. We laughed and talked for hours.

By the end, I understood that food is more than something to eat. It is a way to bring people together. Whether it is a Western meal or a Chinese one, the best part is the same: sitting with friends, sharing food, and enjoying the time together.
