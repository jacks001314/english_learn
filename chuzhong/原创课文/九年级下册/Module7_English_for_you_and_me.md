# 原创课文 · 九年级下册 Module 7 English for you and me

> 主题：英语学习与语言；词汇对齐：achieve / including / speaker / boss / secretary / quarter / industry / zero / Indian / type

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** English is so important these days. Have you ever been to an English corner?

**Lily:** Yes, I have. I talk to other speakers there, and it helps me a lot.

**Tom:** What do you want to achieve with your English?

**Lily:** I want to work in a big company one day and travel around the world.

**Tom:** That sounds great. Many people in business speak English every day.

**Lily:** I know. English belongs to everyone, and there is no reason to be afraid of it.

## 短文（Reading）

### English for You and Me

English is one of the most widely spoken languages in the world. It is used in business, in science, on the Internet and in almost every part of our lives. More people speak English than any other language, and more and more people are learning it every day.

English was once the language of a few countries. Today, it belongs to the whole world. A speaker in India, a boss in America and a secretary in England all use the same words, but in their own ways. In this sense, English is not just for the English. It is for you and me.

Why is English so important? First, it helps us in our work. In many industries, people use English to write letters, send emails and talk to partners from other countries. If you can speak English, you have more chances to get a good job and to meet people from around the world.

Second, English helps us to learn and to dream. Most of the information on the Internet is in English. Great books, films and ideas travel from one country to another through this language. When we learn English, we open a door to a bigger world.

Of course, learning English is not always easy. We have to remember many words and learn the grammar. But with practice and a lot of patience, we can achieve our goals. Some people learn quickly; others need more time. The important thing is to keep going.

There is no magic to learning a language. There is only effort, a little courage, and the wish to understand others. If we use English with open minds and friendly hearts, it will always serve us well. English is a bridge, and it is for everyone.
