# 原创课文 · 七年级下册 Unit 2 Go for it!

> 主题：目标与行动；词汇对齐：last but not least / marathon / however / still / metre / line / breath / hold your breath / would / confident / stomachache / shall / either / choice / seem / someone / neither / cheer / cross / lifelong / dream / past / record / none / member / moment / if / possible / purpose / raise

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** There is a school marathon next month. I want to run it.

**Leo:** A marathon is a long race - over forty-two kilometres! Are you sure?

**Mia:** Yes. It seems hard, but I have a dream. I want to cross the finish line. If I train every day, it is possible.

**Leo:** You are so confident. I will cheer for you at the finish line.

**Mia:** Thank you. It is a lifelong goal for me, not just a one-day race.

**Leo:** Go for it, Mia! Last but not least, remember to enjoy the race.

## 短文（Reading）

### Go for the Finish Line

Last year, my friend Mia watched a marathon in our town. The runners were strong, and the crowd cheered as they ran past. Mia stood by the road and felt her heart beat faster. That day she made a choice: one day, she would be one of those runners.

At first, Mia could not run far. After a few hundred metres, she was tired and out of breath. Her legs hurt, and her stomach ached. Sometimes she wanted to stop. But she did not give up. She told herself that a long race begins with a single step.

Every morning before school, she ran along the river. Her father rode his bike next to her. "Hold your breath and run slowly," he said. "Do not think about the record. Just keep going."

Soon, Mia could run further than ever before. She joined a running club, and made new friends who had the same dream. They trained together and helped one another. Sometimes she would lose a race, but she never lost her purpose. Every time she was tired, she would think of the day she first watched the runners and decided to go for it.

On the day of the race, the town came to watch. Mia stood at the start line, took a deep breath, and ran. The crowd shouted her name. At last, she crossed the finish line. It was the best moment of her whole life.

We all have dreams. The secret is not to wait, but to start. If you set a purpose and work hard, nothing is impossible. So go for it - your finish line is waiting for you.
