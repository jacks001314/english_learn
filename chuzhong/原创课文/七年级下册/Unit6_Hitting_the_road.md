# 原创课文 · 七年级下册 Unit 6 Hitting the road

> 主题：旅行与出发；词汇对齐：airport / burn / expect / deep / lemon / reply / mouthful / surprisingly / mask / normal / ancient / further / look forward to / general / in general / curious / cultural / understanding / main / prefer / interest / places of interest / hotel / comfortable / postcard / waterfall / south / powerful / thunder / painting

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Where are you going for the holiday, Anna?

**Anna:** I'm going to the south, to see a big waterfall. I've been looking forward to it for a long time.

**Tom:** How are you getting there?

**Anna:** By train. And then I'll stay in a small hotel near the waterfall.

**Tom:** What do you like most about travelling?

**Anna:** I'm curious about different places. I like to visit places of interest and learn about ancient cultures.

**Tom:** Me too. In general, travel makes us see the world in a new way.

## 短文（Reading）

### Hit the Road

There is a special feeling when you step out of the airport, or when the train starts to move. Your trip has begun. Your heart is full of excitement, and you cannot wait to see what is waiting for you.

Why do we travel? In general, we travel because we are curious. We want to see a distant waterfall, to stand in front of an ancient temple, or to swim in a warm sea in the south. We look forward to visiting famous places of interest and hearing their stories. We also want to taste the local food, buy a colourful postcard, and meet people who live differently from us.

Travel is also a way to understand the world. When we see how others live, we learn more about our own lives. We learn to be patient and kind. We learn that a normal day in another place can feel like a wonderful dream.

Sometimes travel is not easy. The road may be long, and the weather may change. A powerful thunderstorm can make the sky dark, and the bus may be late. But these small surprises become the best stories we tell our friends. The mouthful of strange food we were too shy to try, the map we could not read, the painting we loved in a museum - these are the things we remember best.

So do not be afraid to leave home. Pack a small bag, take a deep breath, and go. A trip does not have to be far or expensive. Even a walk to the next town can be a wonderful adventure.

The road is calling you. Hit the road, and let the world surprise you.
