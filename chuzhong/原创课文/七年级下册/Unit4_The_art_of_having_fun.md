## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** I'm bored. There's nothing to do this weekend.

**Mia:** Imagine how much fun you could have. Why not read a novel or a magazine?

**Tom:** I read all the time. I want to do something different.

**Mia:** Then let's explore the park. In spring, you can see butterflies and insects. We might even discover something new.

**Tom:** That sounds better than being alone at home.

**Mia:** It's a good feeling, isn't it? Real fun is something you make yourself.

**Tom:** Let me invite my cousin too, and we can have an unforgettable day.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Art of Having Fun

It is easy to say "I'm bored." Sometimes a day feels dull, and everything seems the same. But fun is not something that falls from the sky. It is something we learn to make, and this is an art.

Some people find fun in small things. They read a novel in the afternoon light or look through an old magazine. Others get up and go outside. They ride a bike, play a game, or explore the woods to discover a new insect, a butterfly or a quiet pond.

Fun does not need expensive toys or a crowded place. It can come from simple tools. On a sunny day, you can watch the sky. On a rainy one, you can listen to the sound of the rain and draw a picture. You can invite a friend to help with the housework and turn work into play.

The best memories are often the simplest ones. I still remember a summer evening when we sat on the grass and talked until it was dark. We did not do anything special; we just talked and laughed. That evening is an unforgettable part of my life.

Sometimes, being alone is also a part of fun. When you are by yourself, you can dream a little. You might imagine a prince on a mountain or a friendly monster in the forest. Your mind is a wonderful and youthful place.

So the next time you feel that a day is boring, remember two things. First, fun is inside you. And second, life is a story that you are writing every day. You are free to fill it with laughter, ideas and adventure.
