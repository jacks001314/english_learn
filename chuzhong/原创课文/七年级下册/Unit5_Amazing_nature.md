# 原创课文 · 七年级下册 Unit 5 Amazing nature

> 主题：自然奇观；词汇对齐：plateau / cloud / soft / brightly / lotus / sky / calm / above / express / grassland / field / few / present / proud / likely / local / designer / guide / describe / landscape / common / Mount / tide / exhibition / tour / lungfish / lung / scientist / behaviour / land

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Anna:** Have you ever seen the sea?

**Leo:** Yes. I stood by the water and watched the tide come in. It was calm and wonderful.

**Anna:** I'd like to see a mountain. My uncle is a tour guide, and he once showed me pictures of a snowy Mount.

**Leo:** The landscape there is amazing. The clouds are soft and bright, and the sky is so wide.

**Anna:** Nature makes me think. It is the best artist in the world.

**Leo:** I agree. It is hard to describe, but you feel it in your heart.

## 短文（Reading）

### Amazing Nature

Nature is the greatest artist in the world. It paints the sky in the morning, and it puts clouds in strange and beautiful shapes. It makes the grass grow green on the wide grassland, and it keeps the land calm and quiet at night.

Our country is full of natural wonders. You can visit a high plateau where the air is thin and the light is bright. You can walk across a cool field and see lotus flowers open in the still water. At the coast, you can watch the tide rise and fall, a dance that has gone on for thousands of years. Near a great Mount, the clouds sit above the peaks like soft white snow.

Scientists study these places to learn about the world. They watch the behaviour of animals and the changes of the land. The lungfish is one such animal; it has both lungs and can live on land for a long time. The more we understand nature, the more we see how amazing it truly is.

People also make nature a part of their lives. A local designer might paint a lotus on a piece of cloth. A guide may show visitors an exhibition about the sounds of the forest. A child may proudly present a drawing of the sea to his mother. In this way, nature moves from the outside world into our hearts.

Nature is not something we own. It is something we are part of. If we keep it safe, it will keep us safe, too.
