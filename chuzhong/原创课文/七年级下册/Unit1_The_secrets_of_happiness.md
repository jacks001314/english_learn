## 对话（Dialogue）

**Grace:** Your new school looks great. Do you like it?

**Ben:** Yes, it's exciting, but the best part is meeting new friends.

**Grace:** What makes you happy?

**Ben:** Little things. A walk in the park, a warm cup of chocolate after lunch, or a sunny day.

**Grace:** I agree. But some people think only money can bring happiness.

**Ben:** A rich life is not always a happy one. Happiness is a choice, and it starts in our hearts.

**Grace:** That's true. I feel happy when I help others and when I share something with my friends.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Secrets of Happiness

What is happiness? It is a word we all know, but it is not easy to describe. Some people say that happiness comes from money or success. Others think it is mostly a feeling we are born with. In my opinion, happiness is a choice we make every day.

Happiness is often found in small things. A child smiles when he opens a piece of chocolate. A girl feels warm when her mother touches her hand. A man is glad when he returns to his quiet little town after a long trip. Even in a poor village, where the winter is cold and the light is sunless, people can be happy because they have each other.

Sometimes we forget that the most ordinary days can be the happiest. A bowl of watery soup may not taste special, but it is very welcome in a freezing winter. A simple meal with your family can make you feel complete. A short walk in the morning, a game with a friend, the beautiful sound of rain on the window - these are the simple secrets of happiness.

Of course, the happy life is not perfect. There is always a dark side, and we all have problems. But when we choose to look at the bright side, we understand that we are rich in many ways.

So do not wait for a special day to be happy. Give a smile, share a kind word, and be thankful for what you have. Happiness is not something that happens to us. It is something we choose, again and again.
