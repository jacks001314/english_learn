# 原创课文 · 七年级上册 Unit 4 Time to celebrate

> 主题：节日与文化符号；词汇对齐：unusual / university / treat / realise / dumpling / whole / cucumber / hang / balloon / lantern / dish / joke / shocked / laugh / pork / round / shape / reunion / piece / knife / smell / fill / Christmas / waste / throw away / pollution / plastic / celebrate / meal

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Anna:** Are you excited about the Spring Festival, Li?

**Li:** Very much. It's the most important festival in my country.

**Anna:** What do you usually do?

**Li:** My whole family comes home for a reunion. We hang red lanterns and make round dumplings together.

**Anna:** Do you give presents?

**Li:** Yes, and we give children red envelopes. We also share a big meal on the night before the festival.

**Anna:** It sounds wonderful. What is your favourite part?

**Li:** Sitting with everyone and laughing. That is what celebration really means to me.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Family Reunion

Every culture has its own festivals, and each one tells a story about the people who celebrate it. For me, the Spring Festival is more than a day off. It is the time when a whole family comes together.

Before the festival, the house is filled with colour. We hang red lanterns on the door, put up paper decorations and buy round dumplings with pork and vegetables. Dumplings have a special shape, because they look like small pieces of moon, a symbol of reunion.

In the kitchen, everyone has a job. My mother makes the filling, my father rolls the dough, and I help stuff the dumplings. The kitchen smells wonderful. We cook a big meal for the night before the festival, and the whole table is full of dishes.

On the evening of the festival, we sit around the table and eat. The television is on, but nobody really watches it. We talk about life, we laugh about old jokes, and we remember the people who are far away. At midnight, fireworks fill the sky, and we wish each other a happy new year.

Sometimes I think we celebrate too much, and we waste food or use too much plastic. That is not good for the planet. A good celebration should also be a kind one.

Each generation seems to celebrate in a different way, but the heart of the festival never changes. It is about love, family and hope. When we sit together, we understand what we are celebrating, and we realise that home is the best place of all.
