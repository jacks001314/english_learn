# 原创课文 · 七年级上册 Unit 5 Fantastic friends

> 主题：动物与我们（友谊、救助）；词汇对齐：pigeon / surprise / knocking / droppings / boring / feed / madly / scary / research / recognise / themselves / mirror / itself / maybe / several / kilometre / get lost / speed / amazing / enough / beaver / wolf / engineer / hero / rescue / earthquake / missing / save / dead / dodo

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Did you know that some animals can help people?

**Lily:** Yes! I read about a pigeon that carried a message for a long way.

**Tom:** Amazing. Scientists say that pigeons can recognise places and never get lost.

**Lily:** What about dogs? They are famous for helping in an earthquake.

**Tom:** True. After a big earthquake, rescue dogs can find people who are missing. They are real heroes.

**Lily:** I think animals are fantastic friends. They are never boring to be with.

**Tom:** I agree. We should feed them and take care of them, and they give us so much in return.

## 短文（Reading）

### Fantastic Friends

When I was a child, my grandfather kept pigeons on the roof of our house. Every morning, he would open the small door and throw the birds some food. Then they would fly up into the sky and dance in the wind.

One day, he gave me a pigeon and asked me to take it to a place several kilometres away. I was sure the bird would get lost. But when I reached the place and opened the box, the pigeon flew out and turned round. That afternoon, it was already sitting on the roof, waiting for me. I was so surprised that I could not believe my eyes.

Since then, I have been interested in animals and in the amazing things they can do. Pigeons can recognise homes far away. Beavers build riverside homes that are warm and safe. A wolf can travel across great distances to find its family. Nobody teaches them; they seem to know what to do by themselves.

Some animals are also brave helpers. After a big earthquake, rescue dogs find people who are missing under the broken walls. They work without a rest, and they never give up. For the people they save, these dogs are heroes.

Sadly, some animals are not so lucky. The dodo is dead, and many others are in danger. That is why we should respect every living thing. A horse, a bird, a dog or a cat can be a fantastic friend. If we love them and look after them, they will never let us down.
