## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What's your hobby, Alan?

**Alan:** I play the guitar in a band. We play rock music at the school festival.

**Mia:** That sounds exciting! How did you start?

**Alan:** I decided to learn an instrument last year. At first my fingers were weak, but I practised every day.

**Mia:** Do you enjoy it?

**Alan:** Yes! When I play on the stage, everybody smiles. It's more than fun.

**Mia:** What about you? Do you have a hobby?

**Alan:** I also like paper-cutting. It's a traditional Chinese art. I hold the paper on the table and cut it carefully.

**Mia:** You can do many things. That's really cool.

## 短文（Reading）

### More Than Fun

Everybody has a hobby. Some people like sports, some enjoy painting, and some love music. A hobby is not only a way to have fun; it can also teach us important things about ourselves.

Last year, I joined a music club and learned to play the guitar. At first, it was hard. My hands were weak, and I could not hold the strings well. The sound I made was terrible. But I decided to keep going. Every afternoon, I found a quiet place and practised for an hour.

One day, our band was asked to play at the school festival. I was very nervous as I walked onto the stage. Then I began to play, and suddenly the music took over. I stopped thinking about the people in front of me. I just focused on the notes. The whole hall was quiet, and then the audience began to cheer.

After that day, I understood something. Hobbies are more than fun. They teach us patience and courage. A friend of mine enjoys paper-cutting, and he says the same thing. His hobby connects him to our traditional culture. Another friend loves skating, and another plays volleyball. They are different, but they all feel the same joy.

So find a hobby that you love. Work hard at it, listen to your heart, and you may discover a wonderful world. Your hobby is waiting to wake you up.
