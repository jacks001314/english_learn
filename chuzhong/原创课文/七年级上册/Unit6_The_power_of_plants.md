## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** Look at this little plant. It's only a seed now.

**Leo:** Each seed can grow into a big tree. How does it do that?

**Mia:** A plant makes its own food. It collects light and water and produces sugar.

**Leo:** And it makes oxygen for us to breathe, right?

**Mia:** Yes. Without plants, humans could not live on the earth.

**Leo:** That's amazing. We can also use plants for food and for making paper, even for medicine.

**Mia:** So we should protect the rainforest and every living plant.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Power of Plants

A tiny seed can become a whole forest. This is the quiet power of plants, and it is one of the most wonderful things in nature.

A plant works like a small factory. Its roots find water deep in the soil and send it up through the stem. Its leaves rise into the air and collect light from the sun. From the light, the water and the air, the plant makes its own food. We call this process food-making, and it also gives out oxygen, the gas that every human and animal needs to breathe. Without it, the world would soon become dark and still.

People have used plants for a very long time. We eat their fruits, roots and seeds. We take sugar and healthy products from them. We make paper, wood, cloth and medicine. In a beautiful forest, like the rainforest, the air is fresh, and the plants are part of the culture and stories of the people who live there.

In some places, people use special plants, such as corn silk, to make tea for health. A rose is not only a gift of love; its leaves and petals hold useful oils.

Though we cannot see them work, plants are always busy. They do not rest as we do. They grow slowly, day and night, in the light and in the dark.

So the next time you plant a seed, think about it. In one small seed there is the power to give food, to make air, and to heal the body. That is why we must look after the plants, the soil and the green world around us. The power of plants is the power of life itself.
