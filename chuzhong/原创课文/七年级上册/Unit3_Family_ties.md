# 原创课文 · 七年级上册 Unit 3 Family ties

> 主题：亲情与代际理解；词汇对齐：silent / along / mountain / road / handsome / strict / follow / touching / son / serve / area / absent / seldom / position / carry / across / memory / tear / growth / hide / care / hug / kiss / marry / pick up / actress / change / race

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lucy:** You look quiet today. Is everything all right, Jack?

**Jack:** I am thinking about my father. He works in another city and comes home only twice a year.

**Lucy:** That must be hard for both of you.

**Jack:** Yes. When I was little, I thought he was strict and seldom smiled. Then I grew up and understood.

**Lucy:** What changed your mind?

**Jack:** One day he saw me from far away and ran across the road to hug me. I saw a tear in his eye.

**Lucy:** That's really touching. Our family is always with us, even when we are far apart.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Man on the Road

My father was a postman. Every morning, he got up before the sun and walked along the mountain road to serve the people in the small village. The road was long and difficult, but he never complained.

My father was a quiet man. He seldom spoke, and to me he seemed strict. When I made a mistake, he would not shout. He would only look at me with serious eyes, and then carry on with his work. I thought he did not care about me, and I hid my feelings inside.

As I grew older, I began to understand. He worked hard because he loved us. He gave his strength and his time to give us a better life. When he came home, he would pick up my little brother with a laugh, even though he was tired. His hands were strong and warm.

One rainy day, I was walking home from school when I saw him on the road. He was carrying a heavy bag, and his old coat was wet. When he saw me, he ran across the road and opened his arms. We stood there in the rain, and he hugged me. I felt the tears I had hidden for so long.

Now I understand what family means. It is not about big words or grand gifts. It is about the small things we do for each other every day. A father's love is silent, but it is like the mountain that stands still and strong.
