## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lily:** Hi, I'm Lily. This is our first day at middle school. How do you feel?

**Tom:** A little nervous, to be honest. I'm still thinking about my primary school.

**Lily:** Me too. But a new start is a new journey.

**Tom:** What do you think is the most important thing in the first week?

**Lily:** I think we need a good plan. We have lots of homework and different projects.

**Tom:** Do you have any advice for me?

**Lily:** Yes. Write down your tasks and check them every day. If you make a mistake, don't worry. Just point it out and try again.

**Tom:** That's a good idea. Let's help each other, and then school life will be a lot easier.

## 短文（Reading）

### A New Start

September is a special month. It is the time when a new school year begins. For many of us, it is also the start of a new journey in our lives.

At primary school, we were the oldest students. Now, at middle school, we are the youngest again. Sometimes we feel a little worried. We have new teachers, new friends and new subjects. The first week is full of problems: we forget the classroom, we cannot find the dining hall, and we often have too much homework.

In fact, all these problems are easy to solve. The most important thing is to make a plan. Make a list of your tasks for each day. Write down your homework the moment the teacher gives it. Then finish one thing at a time.

It is also useful to remember that nobody is perfect. If you make a mistake, do not be afraid. Point it out to your teacher, and learn from it. A kind friend is always helpful, too. When someone feels lost, a polite word can make the whole day better.

This is the secret of a good new start: stay calm, work hard, and help each other. With a little plan and a lot of practice, middle school will be a wonderful time for all of us.
