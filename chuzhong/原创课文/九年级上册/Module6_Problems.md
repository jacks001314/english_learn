## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** You look worried. Is something wrong, Lily?

**Lily:** I have a problem. I want to play the guitar, but my parents say I should focus on my schoolwork.

**Tom:** Can you find a way to do both?

**Lily:** I think I can. If I practise for thirty minutes after homework, I will not fail my exams.

**Tom:** That is a good plan. If you show them you are serious, they may reconsider.

**Lily:** You are right. Helping in the community is also a good way to learn new skills.

## 短文（Reading）

### Solving a Problem

We all have problems. Some are big, and some are small. A problem can be about school, about work, or about our family. The important thing is not to run away from it, but to deal with it in a good way.

Last term, my friend Lily had a problem. She loved music and had got into the habit of playing the guitar every evening. But her parents were worried about her exams. They told her that if she failed the test, she would have to give up music. Lily was very unhappy, and she did not know what to do.

One day, she came round to my house and told me everything. "I do not want to lose my music," she said. "But I am afraid of failing my exams."

We talked for a long time. I said, "Maybe you should try out a new plan. Instead of playing for two hours, play for half an hour after you finish your homework. In this way, you can still enjoy your hobby, and your parents will see that you are serious."

Lily took my advice. She made a list of her schoolwork and worked on it first. Then she practised the guitar for a short time. A month later, her marks in the exam were very good. Her parents were pleased, and they let her play music again.

Lily learned something important. A problem is not a shame. It is a chance to think and to grow. If we consider the reasons and speak to the people we trust, we can always find a way out.

If you have a problem today, do not worry too much. Take a deep breath, make a plan, and ask for help. In the end, you may become stronger than before.
