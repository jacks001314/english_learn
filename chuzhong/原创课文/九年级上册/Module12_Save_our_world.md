# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 12 Save our world

> 主题：环保（reduce, reuse, recycle）；词汇对齐：factory / pollute / recycle / waste / enemy / crop / kill / oil / less / hopeless / china / divide / plastic / policy / reuse / bottle / throw away / repeat / cloth / ton / tons of / rubber / recycling / rapid

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** Have you seen the new film about the environment?

**Tom:** Yes. It shows how a factory pollutes the river, and how a lot of plastic is thrown away every day.

**Mia:** It makes me think. We can reduce, reuse and recycle. For example, we can use a bottle again instead of throwing it away.

**Tom:** That is a good start. If everyone does a little, the world will be saved.

**Mia:** You are right. It is not hopeless. We just need to act, and to act now.

## 短文（Reading）

### Save Our World

Our planet is a wonderful place, but we are giving it a hard time. Every day, factories send smoke into the air, cars fill the streets, and mountains of waste grow by the side of the road. This is a serious problem, and it is growing fast.

Some people think that nothing can be done. But the truth is that we can help. The best way is to remember three simple words: reduce, reuse and recycle.

To reduce means to use less. We can save energy by turning off the lights when we leave a room. We can use less water when we brush our teeth. We can buy fewer things that we do not really need.

To reuse means to use a thing again. A glass bottle can hold water for many days before it is empty. An old cloth can become a bag or a rag. We can use both sides of a piece of paper, and pass an old book to a younger friend. In this way, we do not throw away things that are still useful.

To recycle means to make new things from old ones. Paper, plastic, glass and rubber can all be made into new products. By recycling, we make less rubbish and we save the earth's resources.

We can also act together in a bigger way. We can ask the government to make good policy, and we can join a recycling group in our town. Even a small change in our daily life can help.

The earth is our only home. It is not too late, but the time is running out. If everyone does a little, together we can save our world.
