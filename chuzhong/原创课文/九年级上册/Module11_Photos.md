## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** There is a photo competition this evening. Are you entering?

**Tom:** You bet! I took a picture of a beautiful sky last week. The feeling was really strong.

**Mia:** I think you are in with a chance to win.

**Tom:** Thanks, but I am still nervous. I have been working on the subject for days, but recently the light has not been good.

**Mia:** Do not worry. Even though there are many photos, I believe yours is special.

**Tom:** I hope so. I will show it to the headmaster tonight.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Moment in the Photograph

When I was a child, my mother gave me a small camera. She said, "A photograph can hold a moment forever." I did not understand her then, but today I do.

Photography is more than a way to take a picture. It is a way to capture a feeling. A good photograph is not just a copy of the world. It shows us the light in the sky, the smile on a face, or the quiet of a street at night. It can make us feel happy, sad, or surprised.

Last month, our school announced a photo competition. The subject was "Life in My Town." I wanted to take a picture that showed the heart of my home.

For days, I walked around with my camera. I saw many beautiful things, but I was not happy with them. Compared with the pictures on the Internet, they seemed nothing special. Then, one evening, I was walking home when the sky turned red and gold. An old man was sitting on the bank with his dog, and the light fell softly on them. I raised my camera and took one picture.

The photo was not perfect. The light was low, and the man was not in the middle of the picture. But it showed something real - a quiet moment of love between a man and his dog.

The next day, the winners were read out. I could not believe my ears when I heard my name. I won the first prize!

At the ceremony, the headmaster said that the best photographs are not the most beautiful, but the most honest. They protect a true feeling against the passing of time. I understood then what my mother meant. A photograph is not only an image. It is a memory that we can hold in our hands.
