# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 2 Public holidays

> 主题：公共节日；词汇对齐：found / since then / flag / until / off / all kinds of / vacation / take a vacation / season / kid / have fun / band / UK / as soon as / fourth / sixth / seventh / eighth / ninth / tenth / twelfth / twentieth / among / speech

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Anna:** What is your favourite public holiday, Jack?

**Jack:** The National Day holiday. We have a week off, and everybody has fun.

**Anna:** What do you usually do?

**Jack:** I visit my grandparents in the countryside. As soon as I get there, my cousin and I go out to play.

**Anna:** On public holidays, many people take a vacation or watch the flag rise.

**Jack:** Yes. Each season has its own holiday, and there is always a reason to celebrate.

## 短文（Reading）

### Days for Everyone

Every country has its own public holidays. Some are about a great event in history, and others are about the seasons or the harvest. They are days when people do not go to work or school, and the whole country stops to rest and to have fun.

In the UK, there are holidays that go back a long time. People stay at home, cook special food, and watch the flag rise over the square. Children have a vacation, and families travel from one place to another. There are all kinds of interesting things to do.

In my country, the most important public holiday is the National Day. Since then, every year on this day, we remember the start of our country. The streets are full of flags, and the speeches on television tell the story of our people. At night, the sky is bright with fireworks. We take a vacation to be with our families and friends.

Many holidays celebrate the changing seasons. In spring, people welcome the new year and the new plants. In summer, they enjoy the long days and go to the sea. In autumn, they thank the earth for the harvest. In winter, they rest by the fire and wait for the quiet snow.

Most of the holidays in a year come from old traditions, but they are still alive today. On these special days, old and young sit together, and the same old stories are told again.

A holiday is a gift. It gives us time, and time is the best present of all. So when the next holiday comes, put down your books and enjoy the moment with the people you love.
