# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 7 Great books

> 主题：名著（被动语态）；词汇对齐：discuss / thinker / wise / review / influence / sense / make sense / by the way / suppose / well-known / adventure / get into trouble / run away / escape / cave / dead / for a time / neighbour / funeral / surprised / alive / southern / state / pay for

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What are you reading, Tom?

**Tom:** A well-known novel. It is an adventure story about a boy who runs away and lives on a river.

**Mia:** I have heard of it. Do you like the main character?

**Tom:** Yes, he is a wise and brave boy. He gets into trouble, but he always finds a way out.

**Mia:** Great books are still loved by people today, aren't they?

**Tom:** They are. They make us think, and that is why they are never forgotten.

## 短文（Reading）

### Why Great Books Live On

Some books are read once and then forgotten. Others are loved for a hundred years. These are the great books, and the reason they live on is that they speak to people in every age.

A great book tells a story, but it does more than that. It makes us think about life. When we read an adventure novel, we travel with the hero. When he gets into trouble, we feel his fear. When he escapes from danger, we feel glad. In this way, a good writer makes us understand the world through another person's eyes.

Many great books were written a long time ago, but their ideas are still important in our lives. A wise thinker once wrote about friendship, about honesty and about the choices we make. We still discuss these questions today. When we read such a book, we say that its words make sense to us.

Books can also shape our history. A famous book about freedom, or about the lives of ordinary people, can influence a whole state or a nation. It can be a voice for those who cannot speak. It can be a mirror in which we see ourselves.

Some great books are hard, and some are full of fun and adventure. But all of them have something to give us. They are the treasure of our culture, passed from hand to hand, from parents to children.

So do not be in a hurry. Take a little time, and pick up a great book. A story about a boy on a river, or a man who lives in a cave, may change the way you see the world. A great book is a friend for life.
