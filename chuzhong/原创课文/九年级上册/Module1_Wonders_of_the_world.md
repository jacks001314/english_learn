## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** Have you seen the Great Wall, Leo?

**Leo:** Yes, I have. It is an amazing man-made wonder. I think it is even more than a wall; it is a part of history.

**Mia:** I agree. In my opinion, the best wonders are the natural ones, like the mountains and the sea.

**Leo:** Both are wonderful in different ways. What would you say is the greatest wonder of all?

**Mia:** Maybe the sky at night, when it is clear and full of stars.

**Leo:** Wow, that is a beautiful answer. Let us go and see it together.

## 短文（Reading）

### Wonders of the World

Since the beginning of time, people have been amazed by the world around them. We have looked up at the high mountains, walked beside the deep rivers, and wondered at the beauty of the earth. These great sights are called the wonders of the world.

Some wonders are natural. The Grand Canyon, with its deep walls and shining rocks, is one of them. The mountains of the east, the silver sea and the great forests are all natural wonders. They are silent and huge, full of the power of nature. When we stand below a high cliff, we feel very small.

Other wonders are man-made. The Great Wall of China, the pyramids of Egypt and the ancient bridges and palaces are works of human hands. They took years to build, and millions of people have worked on them. They show what people can do when they work together.

People have different opinions about the greatest wonder. In my opinion, the most wonderful thing is not a single place. It is the way that the sky changes: the clouds that turn grey before rain, the bright light that shines after a storm, and the clear stars that appear at night. This is a wonder we can see every day.

We do not have to travel far to find a wonder. If we take our time and look around, we will find beauty everywhere. The world is full of surprises, and it is waiting for us to discover them.
