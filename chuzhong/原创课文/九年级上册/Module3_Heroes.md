# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 3 Heroes

> 主题：英雄人物；词汇对齐：including / medal / attend / abroad / once again / doctor / degree / whatever / give up / amazing / will / victory / simply / Canadian / sick / soldier / treat / war / wound / die for / wounded / realise / dying / care

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Lucy:** Who is your hero, Ben?

**Ben:** My grandfather. He was a soldier, but he became a doctor after the war.

**Lucy:** Did he study abroad?

**Ben:** Yes. He went to Canada and got a degree. But when he came back, he found the people in his village were sick and had no doctors.

**Lucy:** So he stayed and helped them?

**Ben:** He did. He treated the sick for the rest of his life. My grandfather was an amazing man, and he never gave up.

## 短文（Reading）

### A True Hero

We often think of heroes as strong and famous people on the news. But a real hero may be a neighbour, a teacher, or even a member of our own family.

My grandfather was such a hero. He was born in a small village. When he was young, a war broke out, and he became a soldier. He fought bravely, and he saw many of his friends die for our country. When the war ended, he decided to become a doctor to help people who were sick and wounded.

There was no medical school near his home, so he travelled far away to study for a degree. He worked hard, and he once again went to the battlefield, this time as a doctor. He treated the wounded with care, and he never gave up, even when the work was hard.

After the war, he could have lived a quiet life in the city. Instead, he chose to return to the countryside. He knew that the villagers had no doctor, and he wanted to help them. He opened a small clinic and treated the sick for many years. The people loved him, and they called him "our doctor."

My grandfather did not win a medal, and he was not famous. But he was a hero in the truest sense. He realised that the greatest victory was not winning a fight, but saving a life. Whatever he did, he did with love.

I want to be like him. I want to help people, and I want to have the same simple courage. That, to me, is what makes a hero.
