# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 4 Home alone

> 主题：独自在家（让步状语从句）；词汇对齐：platform / meeting / miss / shut / lock / simple / anybody / clock / ring / passenger / address / text / text message / couple / a couple of / actually / manage / unhappy / turn off / order / be worried about / business / on business / sofa

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Tom:** Where are your parents, Lucy?

**Lucy:** They are away on business. I am home alone for a couple of days.

**Tom:** Do you like it?

**Lucy:** At first it was fun. But this morning I missed the bus, and I was late for school.

**Tom:** I see. It is not as simple as it looks.

**Lucy:** You are right. Although I can manage most things, I still feel a little lonely at night.

**Tom:** Make sure you lock the door and turn off the lights before you sleep.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Few Days Alone

This week, my parents went on business, and I stayed home alone. At first, I was very happy. I thought it would be a simple and easy life. I could stay up late, eat what I wanted, and watch television all day.

But I soon learned that living alone is not so easy. On the first morning, the alarm clock did not ring, and I woke up late. I ran to the station, but I had already missed the train. I was late for school, and my teacher was not happy with me.

At home, there was more to think about. I had to lock the door, cook my own food, and do the housework. A couple of times, I did something wrong. Once, I put the laundry in the washing machine and the phone rang while I was not looking. The water was everywhere.

My parents called me every evening. "Are you all right?" they asked. "Are you eating well? Did you turn off the gas?" They were worried about me.

Although I had a lot of freedom, I slowly found that I missed them. I missed my mother's cooking and my father's stories. The house was quiet, and I felt unhappy. The sofa felt empty without them.

At the end of the week, my parents came home. I was so glad to see them that I ran to the door.

I learned an important lesson. Being home alone is a way to grow up, but it is also a way to understand how much our families do for us. Freedom is good, but love is even better.
