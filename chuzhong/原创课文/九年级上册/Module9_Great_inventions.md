# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 9 Great inventions

> 主题：伟大发明（被动语态）；词汇对齐：borrow / put up / website / mail / textbook / mainly / thousands of / page / electronic / technology / powerful / memory / full / fix / instructions / lend / properly / look through / printing / at a time / by hand / development / trade / result

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** What is the greatest invention in your opinion, Leo?

**Leo:** I think it is printing. Before it, books were made by hand, and few people could read.

**Mia:** That is true. Look at us now - I can read a book or look at a website on my phone.

**Leo:** Yes, electronic books are powerful, but it is still nice to hold a real book in your hand.

**Mia:** I agree. Technology changes our lives, but some traditions are worth keeping.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Power of Invention

Look around you. The lights in the room, the pen on the desk, and the computer in front of you - all of these are inventions. An invention is an idea that becomes a thing, and it can change the way we live.

Some inventions changed the world more than others. Printing is one of them. Before printing, a book was copied by hand, page by page. It took a long time, and only a few people could read. Then printing was invented. Thousands of books were made quickly, and knowledge travelled from one place to another. Schools grew, and more people learned to read.

In our time, new technology changes our lives almost every day. We borrow books from the library, but we can also read them on a small electronic screen. We send mail through the Internet. We can look through a website, find the answer to a question, and speak to a friend on another side of the world.

Inventions also come from our questions. A person asks, "Can I do this in a better way?" Then they work on an idea, make a plan, and test it. If the result is good, the idea becomes a tool. If it is not, they try again.

Of course, there are also problems. Sometimes we use too much energy, or we forget to be careful with our things. A good invention should be used properly.

The world of inventions is full of surprises. One day, you may have an idea that helps many people. Look around, ask questions, and do not be afraid to try. The next great invention is waiting to be born.
