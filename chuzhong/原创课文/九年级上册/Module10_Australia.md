# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 10 Australia

> 主题：澳大利亚（定语从句）；词汇对齐：central / according to / magical / height / sailing boat / northeast / sheep / hat / keep / keep sb./sth. away / fly / scissors / cut sth. off sth. / wool / diary / keep a diary / hate / ant / brush / at the time / be surprised at / period / period

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Mia:** Where did you go last summer, Tom?

**Tom:** I went to Australia. It is a magical place with a lot of beautiful sights.

**Mia:** What did you see there?

**Tom:** I saw many sheep on the hills, and I even took a sailing boat on the sea. I kept a diary every night about my trip.

**Mia:** That sounds amazing. What was the best part?

**Tom:** I think it was the mountains in the northeast, where the old rocks reach a great height.

**Mia:** Wow, I hope I can go there one day.

## 短文（Reading）

### My Trip to Australia

Last summer, my family travelled to Australia, a country in the southern part of the world. It is a huge land with both modern cities and wild nature.

Australia is famous for its open land. In the central part of the country, the ground is red and dry. Further north and east, there are green hills and mountains that reach a great height. We drove for hours and hours, and we saw farm after farm. The fields were full of sheep, and sometimes we saw a single sheep in the middle of the road, looking at us with a funny face.

One of my favourite days was when we took a sailing boat. The wind was strong, and the sea was blue. I stood at the front of the boat and felt the cool air on my face. It was a magical moment.

At night, I would sit with a diary and write about everything I had seen. I wanted to remember the smell of the land, the sound of the birds, and the feeling of being far from home. I had to be careful, though, because the flies and ants were everywhere. I kept them away with a net and a wide hat, and I was surprised at how quickly they came back.

In the city, I saw the famous bridge and the tall tower. I learned about the old culture of the people who lived there for thousands of years before us.

I came home with a lot of new ideas. Australia is more than a place on the map. It is a beautiful and different world. If you ever have the chance, go and see it for yourself.
