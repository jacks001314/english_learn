## 对话（Dialogue）

**Jack:** The school sports meeting is next week. Are you going to take part?

**Mia:** Yes. I will run in the race and try to break the school record.

**Jack:** Good luck. I prefer the high jump, but I am not very good at it.

**Mia:** Do not worry. You just need to practise more. A good method is important.

**Jack:** You are right. The competition is not only about winning; it is also about fair play.

**Mia:** That is what sport really stands for - teamwork, effort and respect.

## 短文（Reading）

### The Spirit of Sport

Every year, our school holds a sports meeting. The students run, jump, race and cheer. It is a day full of excitement and energy, and everyone wants to win.

Last year, I watched the final race. The athletes ran as fast as the wind, and the crowd was on its feet. At the last moment, one runner fell. The audience feared that the race was over. But the poor girl got back up and kept running. Even though she came last, everybody clapped for her, and her friends ran to help her.

That moment taught me what sport really means. Sport is not only about winning a race or breaking a record. It is about the ability to try, the fair play we show, and the respect we give to each other.

The athletes train hard for months. They learn a method, they practise every day, and they push themselves to do better. A good sportsman or sportswoman also knows how to lose. When they fail, they do not make excuses. They simply work harder next time.

Sport gives us many things. It makes our bodies strong and our minds clear. It teaches us to work with others and to trust our team. It gives us a memory to keep for a lifetime.

The spirit of sport lives in all of us. It is a light that is fair, brave and kind. When we play, we do not have to be the best. We just have to give our best.
