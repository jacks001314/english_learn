# 原创课文 · 九年级上册 Module 5 Museums

> 主题：博物馆与规则；词汇对齐：upstairs / exhibition / rule / against the rules / in trouble / tail / rope / entry / no entry / no good / no wonder / missing / downstairs / punish / communications / physics / chemistry / dig / coal / energy / X-ray / experiment / sand / control

## 对话（Dialogue）

**Amy:** Have you been to the science museum?

**Tom:** Yes, it is my favourite place. There is an exhibition about energy upstairs.

**Amy:** What can you do there?

**Tom:** You can try a lot of experiments. Once I dug into the sand to find real fossils.

**Amy:** Are there many rules?

**Tom:** Yes. You must not touch the rope, and some rooms are no entry. If you break the rules, you will be in trouble.

**Amy:** I see. No wonder they are so careful, because the things are very valuable.

## 短文（Reading）

### A Day at the Museum

My favourite place in the city is the science museum. It is a big, bright building with exhibitions about space, energy, physics and chemistry. It is a place where you can learn by doing, not just by reading.

When you go upstairs, you enter the world of energy. You can see how electricity is made, how a machine works, and how the sun gives us power. You can even stand on a machine and make the lights come on with your own movement.

Downstairs, there is a special room for experiments. Here, you can mix things, test the weight of an object, and look at an X-ray. My favourite activity is digging into the sand to find old bones and ancient things. I once found a small rock and a real fossil. It felt like I was a scientist!

But a museum has rules, and they are important. You must not cross the rope that keeps people away from the valuable things. Some rooms say "no entry" because they are for the workers. If you get into trouble or break a rule, you may be punished or asked to leave. No wonder the teachers tell us to read the signs first.

People in a museum show respect. They speak quietly, keep their hands to themselves, and wait their turn. In this way, everybody can enjoy the wonder of science together.

After my visit, I always go home with a full head of new ideas. A museum is not a boring place. It is a door to the wonderful world of knowledge, and it is open to everyone.
